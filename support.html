Contact: email support@pixsort.ai. We usually reply within a couple of days. Please include your device model and iOS version, and a short description (or screenshot) of the issue.

Frequently asked questions

Does PixSort change or delete anything in Apple Photos?

No. PixSort only reads your library to import private copies. It never deletes, moves, or edits anything in the Photos app — your originals are always safe there.

Where are my photos stored, and does anything get uploaded?

Everything stays on your device (and your own iCloud, if you use it). Nothing is uploaded to us or shared with anyone. All AI runs on-device.

Why does PixSort use extra storage?

Because it keeps its own copies of what you import, that media exists twice — once in Apple Photos and once in PixSort. You can import at a space-saving "reduced resolution" to use less space, and you can delete the originals in the Photos app yourself once you're comfortable.

How do I organise photos?

Imports land in the Inbox. File them into albums to clear it out. A photo can live in several albums with no extra copies. Use Search to find things in plain English, and tag people or objects.

How do I hide private albums?

Set a passcode in Settings, then hide an album — it disappears from the app until you unlock.

How do I get photos back out?

Use Export to save lossless copies to Files (iCloud Drive, a USB drive, or any provider) or share via AirDrop, Messages and Mail. Your Apple Photos originals also always remain available.

I forgot my hidden-album passcode.

For your privacy the passcode isn't recoverable. You can remove it in Settings and set a new one; your photos remain, and (since Apple Photos is untouched) your originals are always available there.

What does "free to try" mean?

PixSort is free to download and lets you import up to 250 photos and videos. A one-time purchase (not a subscription) removes that limit. Searching, sorting, albums and export work the same on every plan; only importing more is limited. Photos you have already imported are never removed, even if you are over the limit. Deleting a photo in PixSort frees up room.

I bought PixSort before it was free. Do I need to buy it again?

No. If you bought PixSort while it was a paid app, it stays fully unlocked for you automatically. You may need to connect to the internet the first time you open the update so the App Store can confirm it. If it ever shows as locked, open Settings → Your plan → Restore Purchases, or contact us.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Install PixSort, sign in with the same Apple ID, then open Settings → Your plan → Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a purchase is refunded, your existing photos stay, and only importing beyond the free limit is switched off.

Can I import a whole album, or all photos from a date range?

Yes. Tap + in the Inbox (or ⋯ inside an album) and choose "From an Album…" or "By Date Range…". Tip: in the Photos app, search for what you want and add it to a new album, then import that album.

How do I import from a USB drive or the Files app?

Tap + in the Inbox (or ⋯ inside an album) and choose "From Files or a Drive…". Pick a folder (or some photos and videos), and PixSort shows what it found. You can have it file the photos into albums named after their folders. PixSort only reads your files and never changes or deletes them. If the drive is unplugged part-way, the import pauses and keeps its place: plug it back in and tap Resume. A photo and a video with the same name in the same folder are brought in together as a Live Photo.

How do I know my export copied everything?

Settings → Export Everything… (or ⋯ → Export Album… inside an album) → Save to Files…, then pick a destination folder such as a USB drive. PixSort copies straight into it with a live count, then shows "All N copied", or lists any files that didn't make it and why (for example the drive is full, or a file is over 4 GB on a FAT32 drive). Run it again and pick the same folder to finish a partial export: files already there are skipped, and nothing is overwritten.

Why weren't some photos exported?

Photos that are only in hidden albums are never exported. Unhide the album first (Albums, hold the album, Unhide), then export.

If I export and import again, what comes back?

The photos and videos themselves, with their date taken and location (those are stored inside the files). PixSort tags, favourites and hidden-album settings are not stored in the files, so they don't come back; folder names can become albums when you import.

Why is importing slow?

If your photos are stored in iCloud, PixSort has to download each original first, and videos can be large. Keep PixSort open on Wi-Fi and plug in your phone. If an import is interrupted, tap Resume to carry on.
